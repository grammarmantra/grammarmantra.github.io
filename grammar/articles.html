ENGLISH GRAMMAR • ARTICLES

Articles made simple.

Learn how to use A, An and The correctly with simple rules, examples, common mistakes and practical questions.

What are Articles?

Articles are words used before nouns to show whether we are talking about something general or something specific. English has three articles: A, An and The.

Quick idea: A and An are indefinite articles. The is the definite article.

A, An and The at a Glance

Article Type Main Use Example
A Indefinite Before a singular countable noun beginning with a consonant sound. She is a teacher.
An Indefinite Before a singular countable noun beginning with a vowel sound. He is an engineer.
The Definite Before a specific or already identified noun. Close the door.
No Article Zero article Used in many general statements with plural or uncountable nouns. Books are useful.

A and An

We use A and An when we talk about one person or thing in a general or non-specific way.

Use A before a consonant sound

The choice between A and An depends on the sound, not simply on the spelling of the first letter.

She is a doctor.
He bought a car.
She is a university student.

Notice that university begins with a /yu/ sound, so we use a.

Use An before a vowel sound

He is an engineer.
She ate an apple.
It was an interesting story.

We also use an before words beginning with a silent h sound.

He is an honest man.

The

We use The when the listener or reader can identify the particular person, place or thing we mean.

1. When something has already been mentioned

I saw a dog. The dog was very friendly.

2. When there is only one in the situation

Please close the door.

3. With specific things

The book on the table is mine.

4. With superlative adjectives

She is the best student in the class.

5. With ordinal numbers

He came in the first position.

When No Article is Used

Sometimes we do not use A, An or The before a noun. This is often called the zero article.

General plural nouns

We often use no article when speaking about things in general.

Dogs are loyal animals.

General uncountable nouns

We often use no article when talking about an uncountable thing in general.

Water is essential for life.

Languages and school subjects

I am learning English.
Mathematics is difficult for some students.

Important Rules to Remember

1. A / An depends on sound

Choose the article according to the sound at the beginning of the next word, not simply its first letter.

a university • an hour • a European country • an MBA

2. A / An is used with singular countable nouns

Use A or An when referring to one non-specific person or thing.

I saw a bird. • She wants an umbrella.

3. The can be used with singular, plural and uncountable nouns

Use The when the particular noun is identifiable from the context.

The book is on the table. • The books on this shelf are mine. • The water in this bottle is cold.

A, An, The or No Article?

A / An

One, non-specific singular countable noun.

She bought a bag.
He ate an orange.

The

A specific or already identified noun.

She bought a bag. The bag is blue.

No Article

Common with general plural and uncountable nouns.

Books are useful.
Water is essential.

Quick Decision

Ask: Is it singular and non-specific? Use A/An. Is it specific? Use The. Is it general plural/uncountable? Often use no article.

I saw a dog. The dog was friendly. Dogs are loyal.

Common Mistakes

✕ He is a honest man.
✓ He is an honest man.
✕ She is an university student.
✓ She is a university student.
✕ Sun rises in east.
✓ The sun rises in the east.
✕ I like the books. (general meaning)
✓ I like books.
