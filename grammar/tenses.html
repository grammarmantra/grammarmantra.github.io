ENGLISH GRAMMAR • TENSES

English Tenses made simple.

Learn all 12 English tenses with easy rules, sentence structures and practical examples. Understand when to use each tense and avoid common mistakes.

What is a tense?

A tense shows the time of an action or state — whether it happens in the present, happened in the past, or will happen in the future.

The 12 tenses

English has three main time periods — Present, Past and Future — and each has four forms: Simple, Continuous, Perfect and Perfect Continuous.

All 12 English Tenses

01 / PRESENT

Simple Present

Structure
Subject + V1(s/es) + Object
Used for
  • habits and routines
  • facts and general truths
  • regular actions
Example
She goes to school every day.
02 / PRESENT

Present Continuous

Structure
Subject + am/is/are + V-ing
Used for
  • actions happening now
  • temporary situations
  • planned near-future actions
Example
They are studying English now.
03 / PRESENT

Present Perfect

Structure
Subject + has/have + V3
Used for
  • completed actions with present relevance
  • life experiences
  • recently completed actions
Example
I have finished my work.
04 / PRESENT

Present Perfect Continuous

Structure
Subject + has/have been + V-ing
Used for
  • actions continuing until now
  • duration of an ongoing action
Example
She has been studying for two hours.
05 / PAST

Simple Past

Structure
Subject + V2 + Object
Used for
  • completed past actions
  • past events at a definite time
  • past habits
Example
We visited Delhi last year.
06 / PAST

Past Continuous

Structure
Subject + was/were + V-ing
Used for
  • an action in progress at a past time
  • background actions
  • interrupted past actions
Example
I was reading when he called.
07 / PAST

Past Perfect

Structure
Subject + had + V3
Used for
  • an earlier action before another past action
  • showing sequence in the past
Example
The train had left before we arrived.
08 / PAST

Past Perfect Continuous

Structure
Subject + had been + V-ing
Used for
  • duration before a past point
  • an ongoing action that ended before another past event
Example
He had been waiting for an hour before the bus came.
09 / FUTURE

Simple Future

Structure
Subject + will + V1
Used for
  • predictions
  • instant decisions
  • promises and offers
Example
I will help you tomorrow.
10 / FUTURE

Future Continuous

Structure
Subject + will be + V-ing
Used for
  • an action in progress at a future time
  • expected ongoing activities
Example
This time tomorrow, I will be travelling.
11 / FUTURE

Future Perfect

Structure
Subject + will have + V3
Used for
  • an action completed before a future time
  • future deadlines
Example
She will have finished the work by Monday.
12 / FUTURE

Future Perfect Continuous

Structure
Subject + will have been + V-ing
Used for
  • duration continuing up to a future point
  • emphasising how long an action will have continued
Example
By June, I will have been working here for five years.

Remember the basic pattern

Simple → regular/completed fact or action   •   Continuous → action in progress   •   Perfect → action completed before a reference time   •   Perfect Continuous → duration of an ongoing action up to a reference time.

COMPARE THE TENSES

Common Tense Comparisons

Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Present Simple: habits, routines and general facts.

She works in a bank.

Present Continuous: an action happening now or around now.

She is working from home today.

Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Past Simple: a completed action in the past.

I watched the movie yesterday.

Past Continuous: an action in progress at a past time.

I was watching TV when he called.

Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Present Perfect: a past action connected with the present, without a definite past time.

I have visited Delhi.

Past Simple: a completed action at a definite past time.

I visited Delhi last year.

Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

Present Perfect: focuses on the result or completed activity.

She has written three letters.

Present Perfect Continuous: focuses on duration or ongoing activity.

She has been writing for two hours.

COMMON MISTAKES

Watch Out for These Errors

✕ I am knowing the answer.
✓ I know the answer.
✕ He have finished his work.
✓ He has finished his work.
✕ I have seen him yesterday.
✓ I saw him yesterday.
✕ She is living here since 2020.
✓ She has been living here since 2020.
