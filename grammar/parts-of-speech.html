ENGLISH GRAMMAR • BASICS

Parts of Speech

Learn the 8 parts of speech in English with simple definitions, rules and practical examples.

What are Parts of Speech?

Parts of speech are categories of words based on the role they play in a sentence. Understanding them helps you identify how words work and how sentences are formed.

Quick idea: English has eight commonly taught parts of speech: Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction and Interjection.

8 Parts of Speech at a Glance

Part of Speech What it does Example
Noun Names a person, place, thing or idea. Riya lives in Delhi.
Pronoun Used in place of a noun. She is my friend.
Verb Shows an action, event or state. They play football.
Adjective Describes or modifies a noun or pronoun. It is a beautiful flower.
Adverb Modifies a verb, adjective or another adverb. She sings beautifully.
Preposition Shows a relationship between words. The book is on the table.
Conjunction Connects words, phrases or clauses. I tried, but I failed.
Interjection Expresses a sudden feeling or emotion. Wow! That is amazing.

Parts of Speech Explained

1. Noun

A noun is a word used to name a person, place, thing, animal or idea.

Rahul bought a new car.

2. Pronoun

A pronoun is used instead of a noun to avoid repeating the same noun again and again.

Rahul is absent because he is ill.

3. Verb

A verb generally shows an action, occurrence or state.

She writes every day.

4. Adjective

An adjective describes or gives more information about a noun or pronoun.

She bought a new dress.

5. Adverb

An adverb can describe a verb, an adjective or another adverb. It often tells us how, when, where or to what extent.

He runs quickly.

6. Preposition

A preposition shows the relationship of a noun or pronoun with another word in the sentence.

The keys are on the table.

7. Conjunction

A conjunction connects words, phrases or clauses.

I wanted to go, but it was raining.

8. Interjection

An interjection expresses a sudden emotion, feeling or reaction.

Wow! That is a beautiful view.

How to Identify Parts of Speech

To identify a part of speech, don't look at the word alone. Look at the role the word plays in the sentence.

  • Find the noun or pronoun.
  • Identify the verb or action.
  • Check which words describe nouns or pronouns.
  • Check which words modify verbs, adjectives or adverbs.
  • Look for words showing relationships such as place, time or direction.
  • Identify words that connect other words or clauses.
  • Notice words expressing sudden emotion.
