Synonyms — List 1.

Important vocabulary words with meanings and useful synonyms.

20 Important Synonyms

These entries are based on the uploaded synonym source. We can add List 2, List 3 and more later without changing this page.

1

Forbear

To restrain oneself from doing something

Synonyms: Refrain
2

Contemptuous

Showing strong dislike or disrespect

Synonyms: Derisive; Disrespectful; Disdainful
3

Banish

To send someone away from a country as punishment

Synonyms: Expel; Exile; Deport; Relegate; Discard
4

Erroneous

Containing or characterized by error

Synonyms: False; Invalid; Wrong; Mistaken
5

Forsake

To abandon or give up

Synonyms: Abandon; Desert; Discard
6

Impart

To make information known; communicate

Synonyms: Transmit
7

Obliterate

To destroy completely; wipe out

Synonyms: Annihilate; Abolish; Erase; Efface; Eradicate; Destroy
8

Frantic

Wild or distraught with fear or anxiety

Synonyms: Agitated; Distraught; Violent; Berserk; Desperate; Panicky
9

Hurdle

A difficulty or obstacle

Synonyms: Obstacle; Impediment
10

Obligatory

A moral or legal duty or commitment

Synonyms: Compulsory; Mandatory; Binding
11

Commitment

A promise or responsibility that must be fulfilled

Synonyms: Responsibility; Binding
12

Sycophant

A person who flatters someone powerful

Synonyms: Fawner; Flatterer; Adulator
13

Anticipate

To expect or prepare for something in advance

Synonyms: Expect; Predict
14

Companion

A person or animal with whom one spends time or travels

Synonyms: Mate; Comrade
15

Deteriorate

To become progressively worse

Synonyms: Worsen; Decline; Regress
16

Foster

To encourage or help develop

Synonyms: Promote; Nurture
17

Impediment

A hindrance or obstruction in doing something

Synonyms: Obstruction; Obstacle; Barrier; Hurdle; Restriction
18

Luxuriant

Rich and profuse in growth

Synonyms: Abundant; Lush; Flourishing
19

Precedence

The condition of having higher importance or priority

Synonyms: Priority
20

Wander

To move about aimlessly or casually

Synonyms: Roam; Deviate
